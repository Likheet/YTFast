# YTFast beside YouTube Music: what differs

Every known difference between YTFast and music.youtube.com, as a
checklist: about 230 items. Work through it in the order below, one item
(or one small group) at a time, following [README.md](README.md): look at
the real page, change the code, compare the demo at 1280×820, check the
cost (0% CPU idle, memory well under 200 MB), then tick the item and say
how it was tested.

Each item reads: what YouTube Music does ("measured" means
[reference.md](reference.md); "CSS" with a line number means
`target/look/ytm/ytmusic.split.css` as downloaded on 8 October 2026), what
YTFast does now (file and line, as of 8 October 2026: another assistant
was changing the same files, so lines move), the change, and its size
(small: a number or two; medium: one widget or layout; large: a new part
or a rework). Notation used throughout: **W** the window's width, **G**
the menu's width (240 open, 72 closed), **A = W − G**, **S** YouTube's
scroll-bar allowance (12; YTFast has no scroll-bar gutter but keeps the
12 so positions match YouTube Music at the same window width).

Found on 8 October 2026 from YouTube Music signed out (1280×820, and 960
to 1920 wide), its stylesheet, and pictures of the demo
(`target/look/app/*.png`, made with `tools/look/demo.ps1 tour`).

### 3. The page grid

YouTube Music's rule (CSS 6849, 6865, 6873, 6858-6876 for G; measured at 1280: content 828 wide with the menu open, 996 closed, left margin 100), written for YTFast. The app has no browser scroll bar, but keep **S = 12** in the formula: then the content width and every card width equal YouTube Music's exactly, the left margin is 100 / 56 as on the site, and the right side keeps 100 + 12 (or 56 + 12) empty, where the page's scroll bar sits (§7) without covering anything.
